## Patterns & Principles

Anti-brainrot design logic, stated + shown, in launcher terms:

1. **Eliminate first, tune later.** Step 1 is deletion: "remove everything from your phone that doesn't contribute in a meaningful way to the person you want to become" [0:48]. The phone is defined as a **communication + music device** [0:59].
2. **Friction over willpower.** Block installation at OS level (Installing Apps = Don't Allow) so relapse requires a deliberate settings expedition [2:48–3:32]. App Limits as a second layer [3:32–3:46].
3. **Separate by device capability.** Social apps live on the *broken-display previous phone*: "keeping those social media apps there reduces friction on my other devices while costing me zero money and making me use a device that would otherwise not be used at all" [2:11–2:27]. Reading moved to a backlight-less e-ink device so late-night reading is literally impossible [4:52–4:57]. A container app (GBox) groups the "needed but distracting" apps into a sandbox shelf [0:29 / 6:18].
4. **Own your services.** Self-hosted music replaces Spotify: no ads, no AI slop, no data collection, more attention per song, and cheaper [1:09–1:50].
5. **Empty home screen.** After setup: **no icons at all on the home screen** — only a 4-icon dock over full-bleed wallpaper [5:50 / 7:49]. Zero tap targets → zero reflexive opens. (Feed-free by architecture, not by blocklist.)
6. **Monochrome uniformity.** iOS 18 **Tinted icons with the eyedropper picking the wallpaper's most-contrasting color** — "in my case, that color is something between black and white" [7:32–7:41]. One color, one family of glyphs; app identity and brand color disappear.
7. **Dark everywhere.** Dark mode + dark manga wallpapers [6:51]; the only color allowed is wallpaper art he genuinely likes.
8. **Looks ≠ behavior.** Honest self-assessment: "the wallpaper you use on your phone won't change the way you actually use it… just pick something that you truly like" [7:08–7:15]; "I really don't think that the way your phone looks will make any huge difference in the way you use it" [7:58]. Visual design is for enjoyment, not enforcement — enforcement lives in elimination + friction.
9. **Phone as yardstick.** "Because our phones are good at everything, they're not the best at anything. And because they are capable of doing so much, we end up doing nothing." [4:10–4:19] — a task should move to a dedicated device only if the phone is genuinely *inefficient* at it (eye strain, no backlight, broken screen, bad battery).
10. **Do less, not zero.** Full-split across 100 devices is called out as "a dumb way of solving the issue" [6:14–6:19]; the ending reframes the goal as **simplifying**, with a subjective-experience disclaimer [8:28–8:45].

## Concrete Ideas For Zendo Launcher

1. **[UI] Tinted/monochrome icon engine with wallpaper eyedropper** — auto-extract the wallpaper's most-contrasting color and render all app glyphs (and optionally labels off) in one tint; offer warm-gold and neutral presets mirroring the video's two states. Effort: **M**.
2. **[UI] "Dock-only" home mode** — official page style with zero icon slots; drawer/search is the only app entry; enforces the video's emptied home screen as a first-class option rather than a hack. Effort: **S**.
3. **[behavior] Install-gate friction** — a launcher-level "Intention check" before opening the package installer / Play Store: multi-step confirm + optional password/cooldown ("installing requires asking yourself why"). Effort: **M**.
4. **[behavior] Intention interstitial for flagged apps** — on open: 10-second hold or type-why field; mirrors "add friction to the undesired actions" [2:48]. Effort: **S**.
5. **[behavior] App container/"shelf" mode** — an in-launcher sandbox list (GBox analog) where clutter apps live behind one extra deliberate step and are excluded from search by default. Effort: **L**.
6. **[philosophy] First-run intent wizard** — "what do you want this phone to be?" → pick purposes (call/text/music/reading/maps); everything else lands in an *opt-in* library, not the home screen. Effort: **M**.
7. **[UI] Wallpaper-first theming** — full-bleed art presets (monochrome manga line-art style) with luminosity-aware status bar and dock styling; no icon frames. Effort: **M**.
8. **[behavior] App-usage schedules instead of (or above) time limits** — allow hiding entire categories in time windows with the launcher owning the surface (feeds into existing block logic). Effort: **M**.
9. **[UI] Grayscale "focus skin" quick toggle** — launcher-driven monochrome mode for icons+widgets without turning on OS grayscale (partial brainrot-dulling without losing accessibility contrast). Effort: **S**.
10. **[behavior] De-emphasize app identity** — hide badges/notification dots by default; batch notifications behind one "Inbox" row instead of icon badges. Effort: **S**.
11. **[philosophy] "Purpose card" onboarding page** — before enabling any optional feature, show one card explaining the anti-brainrot rationale (e.g., why dock-only, why no badges) — teaches the philosophy locally like the video's chapter structure. Effort: **S**.
12. **[behavior] Usage transparency surface** — long-press a hidden corner to reveal screen-time/launch counts; invisible until sought (data exists for the user, not as a guilt widget). Effort: **M**.
